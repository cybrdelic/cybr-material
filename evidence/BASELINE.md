# Original render evidence

The seventeen PNGs in this directory were copied without modification from
v3.1.1, commit `d8b35a52c80667f88c336c9150f64f82cefcf54f`, before any
generator or geometry edits. The original reproducible source and native
scenes are available at that commit in this repository's history.

New native renders are in `path_traced/renders/`; matched surface-camera
proofs are in `path_traced/controlled_surfaces/`. Final render metadata records
actual Cycles settings and times. Display comparisons are in
`docs/images/comparisons/`. No baseline was reconstructed or image-generated.
