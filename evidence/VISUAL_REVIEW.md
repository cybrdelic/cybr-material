# Visual review of the repair branch

Baseline: all seventeen actual v3.1.1 PNGs were inspected before changing the
generators or geometry. The original bytes remain beside this document.

The revised ten material specimens, ten matched surface proofs, three still
lifes and architectural views were inspected as actual rendered pixels. The
final leather finish and expanded interiors received another inspection after
their revisions. Display derivatives never replace the native RGB16 PNGs.

Observations:

- Timber now follows each cut through its faces and end grain; individual
  boards vary. Exported wood atlases remain a separate finite representation.
- Stone is quieter and finer. Marble carries slab thickness and edge continuity;
  travertine pores no longer have drawn dark perimeter rings.
- Metal specimens expose folded construction and restrained tooling; intact
  finishes do not rely on pervasive random damage.
- Porcelain uses an open glazed shape and exposed foot; plaster uses larger
  trowel structures. Linen has thickness, weave and local resting folds.
- Leather has fine grain and curved thickness. The still-life hide is one
  continuous bent piece. Upholstery welts follow rounded pads, with seat and
  back deformation rather than camouflage-like pale patches.
- The salon rug has body and binding. The exterior includes a ground plane,
  planted trees and an open pergola. The sofa remains a simplified authored
  construction, rather than simulated soft upholstery.
- The library has page blocks, covers, spine details, stacks and bookends;
  its bookshelf uprights terminate cleanly. Titles remain abstract marks.
- The kitchen shows fitted fronts, reveals and seating clearance, a complete
  refrigerator/freezer, sink/faucet, controls, hardware and distinct metals.
  Its wider final camera is disclosed in the review document.
- The parquet has per-board cuts and beveled joints; the brass occupies an
  actual routed recess and the old presentation batten is absent.

This review supports the correction claims, not a claim of measured or
photographic fidelity. Analytic cloth shapes, simple planting and abstract
book titles are retained limitations.
