# Current scope and qualification

The selected registry preserves all 35 identities (10 original plus 25 additions).
Selection, asset availability, numerical validity and appearance acceptance are
separate. A later experiment does not automatically replace a stronger baseline.

## Retained and improved

- Strong original/retained panels remain protected, including the revised wood
  anchors and r5 fleece/carpet. Bark r8 and grass r3 are appearance anchors.
- Reviewed complete molded plastic, LCD/CRT display corrections and the preferred
  clear PETG presentation are recorded by exact source/capture/image provenance.
- The shared renderer supports guided, explicit unfiltered and component-recombined
  capture. Float32 EXR serialization, RGB16 output and evaluated transform snapshots
  have corrected contracts and retained regression evidence.
- Source-generation/inspection infrastructure is exercised end to end for brass
  and polished concrete. Brass native pixels are exact while historic compressed
  PNG bytes differ; concrete native channel bytes match. Neither establishes
  measured physical fidelity or qualifies concrete appearance.

## Still held or incomplete

- Concrete formation/appearance remains unresolved; earlier cast/cut prototypes
  and weak procedural finishes are retained as failed evidence.
- New carpet yarn/core/contact constructions have not improved on r5. Numerical
  improvements and source-ready assemblers are not accepted complete carpets.
- Bark annular-sector/prescribed-seam eigenstrain research is a conditional reduced
  model, not accepted mature-bark morphology. Textile spinning/contact experiments
  remain unqualified for production appearance.
- Porcelain now passes full, macro and second-light review for the narrow continuous
  glaze-lip improvement. Its final source and assumed-history limits are preserved.
  The presentation-only registry delta is retained under pending-porcelain-presentation;
  this frozen main registry has not yet applied that later delta.
- Several selected binary scenes/native map sets require their archived inputs.
  The recovered full integration suite has one asset-dependent leather check blocked
  by its missing local coupon scene; no assertion is weakened to hide that absence.
- Full formation physics, independent measured calibration, every material's
  complete replay, and universal clean-clone portability are not claimed.

## Publication boundary

This is a draft-PR recovery and engineering checkpoint. It includes source, tests,
compact provenance, a curated actual render and exact large-artifact references.
It does not merge, release, deploy or assert that all materials are finished.
Large raw histories omitted from Git are recorded in `SOURCE_EXCLUSIONS.json`
and retained in the corresponding exact recovery archive.

Additional source is retained for hex normal precision, fin material visibility,
opaque PETG thermal-profile and brass-scratch studies. These are bounded
correctness/construction experiments; fin appearance is unresolved and none
replaces a stronger selected complete panel.
