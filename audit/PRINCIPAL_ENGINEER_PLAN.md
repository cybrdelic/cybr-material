# Principal-engineer plan

Originally frozen 2026-10-05; retained as the audit program. Current implementation,
selection and remaining failures are recorded in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md). The old
checkpoint date below is historical and is not a claim that its interrupted
publication completed.

## Objective and honest finish line

Build convincing, inspectable materials from coherent formation and finish state,
with exact transfer into the actual scene. Preserve all 35 identities and usable
selected appearances while replacing weak mechanisms one vertical slice at a time.
Completion requires numerical, structural and clean visual evidence together.
Passing algebra or producing an attractive image alone is insufficient.

## 0. Freeze and separate evidence

The 2026-10-05 checkpoint is immutable recovery evidence. Keep original assets,
selected fleece/carpet r5 and revised wood r7 anchors. Record source/state/scene/
map hashes, SI dimensions, seed/history, geometry and capture settings. Failed
experiments never become defaults merely because their revision number is newer.
Label authored parameters, reduced models and measured calibration distinctly.

## 1. Shared correctness before additional detail

- One consistent SI coordinate and physical scale contract through solver, maps,
  mesh, UVs, tangent frames, PBR and camera. Include negative/reversed UV cases.
- Preserve absolute displacement and coating zero modes. Split resolved geometry
  from unresolved normal/roughness contributions; never count relief twice.
- Verify actual Blender RGB16 reads and evaluated triangle frames. Ideal planar
  identities do not qualify a curved or bevelled production mesh.
- Track force, mass, energy and removal ledgers with independent limiting cases,
  reopening checks, source-grid/time-step/contact-quadrature refinement and error
  tolerances tied to a physical quantity, not a convenient display scale.
- Keep one initial state across controlled interventions. A changed camera, light,
  closure, morphology or field of view invalidates a supposed implementation-only
  comparison unless the changed variable is explicitly the experiment.

## 2. Priority vertical slices

### Metals: brass first, then steel

Freeze pristine same-camera normal-transfer control. Finish moving-contact wear
using force-balanced contact quadrature and conservative deposition/removal.
Validate contact area and field as well as total force; reject pixel-center aliases.
Require source-grid and trajectory refinement, nonnegative pressure, bounded
penetration and physically derived removal. Derive roughness/oxide exposure from
the same history. Keep brush features at their physical scale; do not enlarge them
for a distant camera. Only after brass passes, transfer the contract to steel with
its distinct optical/oxide parameters and repeat the complete gates.

### Concrete: one cast/cut specimen before the whole family

The current 32 mm pair fails the visual/structural gate despite bounded numerical
passes. Build defensible irregular aggregate contacts, packing and cut-surface
intersection before richer shaders. Keep actual particle volume and nonpenetration
checks independent of rounded proxies. Test packing/contact primitives against
independent analytic or optimization references. Model mold interface, binder skin,
gas/void geometry and removal using common state. Inspect cast and cut views at
explicit physical scale, then verify a larger-context specimen. Only after this
slice passes should polished, washed, weathered and board-formed variants inherit it.

## 3. Secondary queues stay bounded

- Leather: finish full-domain refinement and representation transfer before any
  production export or beauty. Tiny residuals on one nonconvex branch are not enough.
- Bark: fix pending ring-system integration shape contract; independently establish
  deformation/fracture relevance before replacing selected structural appearance.
- Textiles: retain preferred appearances; qualify rod/contact mechanics, scale,
  fiber contact and optical closure before changing whole-context instances.
- Plastic, glaze, chain and wire: finish field-to-curved-mesh, seam, tangent and
  sampling checks. A normal-precision improvement does not fix lip scallops or
  missing mechanics. Clear PETG remains unqualified for transparent optics.
- Context scenes: repair workshop presentation-card intrusion and revisit rejected
  courtyard wall appearance. Full catalog coverage does not certify microstructure.

## 4. Explicit gates for each candidate

1. Numerical: independent limits, residuals, accounting, reproducibility and
   documented convergence on the same state. Record failed as well as passing tests.
2. Structural: saved/evaluated mesh, intersections, contact, dimensions, UV seams,
   normals, geometry/map bandwidth and actual shader bindings.
3. Visual: clean native full panel, resolved macro, grazing view and second light;
   compare against the frozen anchor and appropriate references without upscaling,
   artificial sharpening, added grain or hiding defects behind denoising.
4. Integration: reconstruct exact source/state in a fresh workspace; verify scene
   coverage and known limits, then update a selected pointer explicitly.

## Work loop and stop conditions

Choose the single highest-impact verified defect. Write a falsifiable hypothesis,
change the smallest mechanism, run the cheapest discriminating check, then advance
through the remaining gates. Regressions retain the previous selection. Commit
bounded verified corrections with receipts. Do not expand to additional families
while a shared correctness defect invalidates current output. Pause only for a
real authority/resource blocker; keep failed hypotheses recoverable and documented.

This plan does not authorize a release, merge, deployment, measured-material
claim or automatic promotion of any current prototype.
